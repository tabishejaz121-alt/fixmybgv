What this service is

Fix My BGV provides advisory and documentation-support consultation only, intended to help Indian salaried professionals understand and navigate employment-record, exit-document, PF/UAN, and background-verification issues. This includes case review, risk assessment, documentation guidance, strategy support, drafting assistance within scope, and practical next-step guidance based on the facts shared by the client. It is consultation and guidance, nothing more, and nothing beyond what is explicitly included in your selected package is owed to you.

Cancellation & refund policy

What you are paying for

This is an advisory service, work begins as soon as your case is reviewed. Because time and effort are committed at that point, our ability to offer refunds is limited. Below is exactly where that line sits.

You can cancel before review begins

A booking may be cancelled or rescheduled at any point before your case review has started. Message us on WhatsApp with your booking reference, 1 free reschedule is available if requested 12+ hours before your slot.

You cannot cancel once review has started

Once your case review has begun, the booking can no longer be cancelled or refunded, since the work has already been performed.

Message us on WhatsApp with your name and booking date. We respond within 1–2 business days.
