This session helps you understand:
  • check_circle What exactly has gone wrong
  • check_circle Whether the issue is employee-side, employer-side, or mixed
  • check_circle What documentation gaps or risk points exist
  • check_circle What immediate steps to take to avoid making it worse
  • check_circle Whether it's a clarification issue, documentation issue, employer dispute, or genuine compliance risk
  • Employment overlap / service overlap showing in records
  • Dual-employment suspicion or moonlighting concern

  • Company-created PF or employment records without proper joining/salary clarity
  • Wrong date of exit / non-updated exit records
  • Notice-period dispute, resignation non-acceptance, or absconding-related concerns
  • Form 26AS / AIS / salary trail confusion affecting employment history

  • Pre-call case intake so the discussion is focused
  • Review of key documents/screenshots before the call
  • Case diagnosis: issue identification, risk assessment, likely problem areas, recommended next steps
  • Post-call written summary with key issue points, suggested action path, and what to collect or preserve
  • Drafting of declaration letters, HR emails, or labour-office applications
  • Ongoing WhatsApp support beyond the review
  • Follow-up review of multiple developments after the call
  • Legal representation, court filing, or appearance before any authority
  • Tax filing, CA certification, or statutory filing services
