description Exit Documents & Notice Period

Yes. Many people seek guidance precisely because their exit wasn't clean or the documentation wasn't completed properly. The consultation exists to understand the facts of your case, identify the likely risk points, and discuss the most practical next steps based on your available records and circumstances.

You do not need every document ready, but it helps if you can gather whatever is available from the following:

  • Employment timeline with company names and dates
  • Resignation email / communication trail
  • Relieving letter / experience letter / exit-related emails
  • PF/UAN screenshots or service-history details
  • Payslips / salary proof if relevant
  • BGV query email or offer-stage concern, if any
  • Any written communication from the employer related to exit, notice period, or dispute
